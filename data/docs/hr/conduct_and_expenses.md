# Code of Conduct

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 9 hours per day including a one hour break. Employees may work remotely
up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

## Data handling
Employee personal data must never be shared over unapproved channels. Report any suspected data
leak to the security desk within 24 hours.

# Travel and Expenses

## Reimbursement
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of travel with original receipts. Claims above
500 USD need director approval.

## Air travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for longer flights
with prior approval.
