# Replenishment SOP (SnackCo Supply Chain)

## Reorder points
A distribution center (DC) must raise a replenishment order when on-hand stock falls below the
reorder point. The reorder point is average daily demand multiplied by supplier lead time in days,
plus safety stock. Safety stock is 20% of lead-time demand for fast-moving SKUs.

## Expedited shipping
Expedited freight requires approval from the regional supply manager when the order value is above
5,000 USD. For orders below this value the DC planner may approve expedited freight directly.

## Stock-out escalation
If projected stock covers fewer than 3 days of demand, open a P2 ticket with the supply desk. If
projected cover is under 1 day, open a P1 ticket and notify the regional supply manager immediately.
