# Supplier Policy

## Lead times
Standard supplier lead time is 7 days for domestic suppliers and 21 days for imported ingredients.
Hyderabad DC sources packaging from two domestic suppliers with a 5 day lead time.

## Quality holds
Any batch flagged by QA is placed on quality hold and must not be counted as available inventory.
Holds are released only by the QA lead after re-inspection.

## Cold chain
Chilled products must be stored between 2 and 6 degrees Celsius. A temperature excursion longer than
30 minutes requires the batch to be quarantined and a deviation report filed.
